Faculty

The professors of Mayantraset School of Magic are among the most accomplished magical scholars in Southeast Asia. Drawn from centuries of magical tradition, they have dedicated their lives to advancing magical knowledge while guiding generations of witches and wizards through every stage of their education.

Meet the Faculty

Every professor at Mayantraset is a master of their discipline. Many have lived for centuries as Immortal Wizards, contributing to magical research, protecting wizarding society, and preserving the ancient traditions of Southeast Asian magic.
