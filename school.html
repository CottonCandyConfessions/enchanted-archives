Mayantraset School of Magic

Founded in 1521

Southeast Asia's oldest magical institution, educating witches and wizards from across the region for over five centuries. Nestled within the mountains of Northern Thailand, Mayantraset has served as the heart of magical education, scholarship, and cultural preservation throughout Southeast Asia.
