Mayantraset School of Magic

Founded in 1521

Southeast Asia's oldest magical institution, educating witches and wizards from across the region for more than five centuries. Hidden deep within the mountains of Northern Thailand, Mayantraset has long stood as a centre of magical scholarship, cultural preservation, and innovation for the wizarding communities of Southeast Asia.
