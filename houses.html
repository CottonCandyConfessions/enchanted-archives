The Four Houses of Mayantraset

The Four Houses represent four distinct paths through which students grow into complete witches and wizards. Rather than measuring talent, each House nurtures the qualities that come most naturally to its students while encouraging them to learn from one another throughout their years at Mayantraset.

House Singha

"Strength in Service."

Colours: Crimson & Gold

The Singha believe that magic exists first and foremost to protect. Students of Singha are often the first to stand between danger and another person, whether through powerful defensive magic, physical courage, or quiet acts of sacrifice. While outsiders often mistake them for thrill-seekers, Singha students rarely seek glory—they seek responsibility.

To a Singha, courage is not the absence of fear. It is choosing to act despite it.

Many of Thailand's finest Aurors, magical law enforcers, curse-breakers, royal guards, and battlefield healers have come from Singha House. They thrive under pressure, excel in duelling, and are fiercely loyal to the people they love.

Singha House values courage above all else—not the absence of fear, but the strength to face it. Its students are guided by an unwavering sense of duty, placing the well-being of others and the greater good above personal ambition. Loyalty forms the heart of the house, fostering bonds that are steadfast and unbreakable through both triumph and hardship. They hold honour as a sacred principle, striving to act with integrity, fairness, and respect in every choice they make. Above all, Singha teaches that true greatness lies in self-sacrifice—the willingness to stand between danger and those who cannot protect themselves, even at great personal cost.

House Motto
"Stand first. Stand firm."

House Kinnari

"Magic should leave the world kinder than it found it."

Colours: Ivory & Violet

Kinnari believes that creation is the highest form of magic. Artists, musicians, healers, poets, potion masters, enchanters, and magical craftsmen all find a home within its halls. They understand that not every battle is won through force. Sometimes the greatest magic is restoring what has been broken. Kinnari students possess remarkable emotional intelligence and creativity. They often approach problems sideways, finding elegant solutions where others see only obstacles. Many become the magical world's greatest healers, artisans, composers, diplomats, and inventors.

Kinnari House is guided by compassion, believing that kindness is one of the greatest forms of strength. Its students are celebrated for their creativity, approaching magic with imagination, innovation, and an appreciation for artistry in all its forms. They seek beauty not only in the world around them, but also in acts of kindness, self-expression, and the connections they forge with others. Harmony lies at the core of the house, encouraging balance between mind, heart, magic, and nature while promoting cooperation over conflict. Above all, Kinnari values empathy—the ability to understand and share others' feelings—and teaches its students that true magic begins with understanding, healing, and uplifting those around them.

House Motto
"Create what cannot be destroyed."

House Erawan

"Knowledge without wisdom is merely information."

Colours: Sapphire & Silver

Erawan is devoted to understanding. Its students believe every spell has an origin. Every curse has a history. Every mystery has an answer waiting to be discovered. They are scholars before they are spellcasters. Curiosity drives them, but wisdom guides them. While Erawan has produced countless researchers, historians, Unspeakables, magical judges, astronomers, and Ministers, its greatest strength lies not in intelligence alone—but in discernment.

Erawan House is built upon the pursuit of wisdom, encouraging its students to seek knowledge not for personal glory, but to better understand the world and those within it. Natural leaders, they inspire others through confidence, fairness, and the ability to guide with both strength and humility. Curiosity drives every Erawan student to question, explore, and push the boundaries of magical discovery, believing that every mystery holds the potential for growth. They uphold integrity in all they do, remaining honest, principled, and steadfast even when faced with difficult choices. Above all, Erawan values responsibility, teaching that true leadership is measured not by power or status, but by the willingness to serve others and bear the consequences of one's decisions.

House Motto
"Seek truth before power."

House Makara

"The horizon is only the beginning."

Colours: Teal & Bronze

Makara is the House of those who refuse to accept that the edge of the map is truly the end. Adventurers, magizoologists, explorers, alchemists, curse-breakers, inventors, and travellers all flourish here. Makara students are endlessly curious, constantly questioning accepted truths and searching for what lies beyond them. They embrace uncertainty with enthusiasm, believing that every discovery begins with the courage to step into the unknown. Independent by nature, they are adaptable, resourceful, and unafraid to carve their own path, even when no one has walked it before.

Makara House values discovery above all, encouraging its students to venture into the unknown with open minds and fearless hearts. Adaptability is one of their greatest strengths, allowing them to overcome unexpected challenges and thrive in unfamiliar situations. Innovation shapes the way they think, inspiring them to experiment, invent, and challenge conventional magical practices. Curiosity drives every expedition, question, and breakthrough, ensuring that learning never truly ends. Above all, Makara celebrates independence, believing that the greatest journeys are often those taken by individuals willing to trust their instincts, embrace uncertainty, and forge their own destinies.

Of course, they also have an uncanny habit of accidentally uncovering long-forgotten temples, awakening ancient guardians, or returning from expeditions carrying artifacts that absolutely should have been left alone.

House Motto
"Beyond every horizon lies another."

The Four Houses

Though each House follows a different path, none is considered superior to another. At Mayantraset, students are taught that a truly accomplished witch or wizard is not defined by a single virtue, but by learning from all four. A Singha may discover compassion from a Kinnari. A Kinnari may learn resilience from a Singha. An Erawan may find inspiration in a Makara's daring, while a Makara may gain wisdom through an Erawan's patience.

The Houses are not rivals in the traditional sense. While friendly competitions exist throughout the school year, they are designed to encourage growth rather than division. Students attend classes together, share common spaces, collaborate on magical research, and often form lifelong friendships that cross House boundaries.

Every year, the House Cup recognizes not only academic excellence and magical achievement, but also acts of courage, creativity, leadership, kindness, discovery, and service to the school community. The Cup is awarded to the House that best embodies the spirit of Mayantraset itself—not through individual brilliance alone, but through the collective character of its students.

The founders believed that no single House could ever produce the perfect witch or wizard. Instead, greatness emerges when the strengths of all four come together. Courage without compassion becomes recklessness. Creativity without wisdom loses direction. Knowledge without curiosity stagnates. Discovery without responsibility becomes dangerous.

Together, the Four Houses remind every student that magic is strongest when diverse talents, perspectives, and values work in harmony.

"A Singha may save a life.
A Kinnari may heal a heart.
An Erawan may change history.
A Makara may discover the future.

Together, they shape the magical world."
