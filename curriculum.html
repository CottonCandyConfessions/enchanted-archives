Academics

Curriculum

At Mayantraset, every student follows a comprehensive magical education during their first five years, building a strong foundation in spellwork, magical theory, history, and the natural magical world. Beginning in their sixth year, students may tailor their education by selecting advanced elective courses aligned with their talents, ambitions, and future professions.

Core Subjects (Years 1–5)

Spellcasting & Magical Theory

The foundation of magical education, covering wandwork, spell structure, magical principles, and the theory behind successful spellcasting.

Defence Against the Dark Arts

Defensive magic, counter-curses, duelling techniques, protective enchantments, and the recognition of Dark Magic.

Potions & Alchemy

Potion brewing, magical ingredients, transmutation, and the fundamental principles of alchemical magic.

Magical Herbology

The cultivation, identification, and magical properties of enchanted flora from Thailand and across the wizarding world.

Magical Creatures

The study, care, behaviour, and conservation of magical beasts and spirits.

Ancient Magic & Runes

Ancient magical scripts, runic inscriptions, forgotten enchantments, and historical magical civilizations.

Divination & Celestial Studies

Prophecy, astrology, magical astronomy, lunar cycles, celestial magic, and divination techniques.

History of Magic

The history of magical societies, international wizarding relations, magical conflicts, and the evolution of magical law.

Advanced Electives (Years 6–7)

Students entering their sixth year may choose elective courses based on their interests and intended magical careers.

🪷 Sacred Enchantments

Protective charms, talismans, magical wards, blessings, and sacred inscriptions.

⚗️ Advanced Alchemy

Magical transmutation, magical metallurgy, rare materials, and advanced alchemical theory.

🗺 Curse-Breaking & Magical Archaeology

Exploration of ancient temples, forgotten civilizations, cursed ruins, relic recovery, and magical preservation.

🎭 Illusion Magic

Advanced glamour magic, concealment, deception spells, perception manipulation, and illusion-based enchantments.

🌧 Elemental Magic

The relationship between spellcasting and the natural elements, including weather manipulation and elemental affinities.

🏺 Magical Artifact Crafting

Designing and enchanting magical tools, relics, enchanted jewelry, talismans, and magical objects.

🌏 Wizarding Cultures & Languages

Ancient magical languages, regional magical traditions, and the diverse magical cultures found throughout Asia.

🌙 Soul Magic & Magical Bonds (Restricted)

The study of magical oaths, soul bonds, magical contracts, familiar connections, and life-force magic.

🐉 Advanced Magizoology

Magical creature conservation, breeding programs, advanced healing, habitat management, and behavioural studies.

💎 Crystal & Gem Magic

Magical gemstones, focusing crystals, ley-line amplification, crystal enchantments, and mineral magic.

🧭 Magical Navigation & Portkey Theory

International magical travel, enchanted maps, apparition theory, magical transportation networks, and Portkey construction.

🌿 Healing Magic

Diagnostic spells, restorative charms, magical anatomy, emergency magical medicine, and potion-assisted healing.

⚖️ Wizarding Law & Ethics

International magical legislation, magical rights, Ministry law, ethical spellcasting, and judicial systems.

🪞 Mind Magic (Restricted)

Occlumency, Legilimency, dream magic, memory charms, mental defense, and psychological spellwork.

⭐ Astral Magic

Planetary alignments, lunar rituals, eclipses, magical calendars, constellations, and celestial spellcraft.

🖋 Spell Creation & Experimental Magic (Final Year Only)

Original spell design, magical research methodology, controlled experimentation, and spell innovation.

🏛 Politics of the Wizarding World

International Ministries, magical diplomacy, treaties, political systems, and historical conflicts.

🌸 Magical Botany

An advanced continuation of Herbology focusing on extinct magical flora, enchanted ecosystems, and plant-based spellcraft.

🕰 Temporal Magic (Theoretical)

A theoretical study of Time-Turners, paradoxes, historical magical chronology, and temporal theory. Practical manipulation of time is strictly prohibited.

Academic Structure

Years 1–5

  • Students complete the full core curriculum.
  • Practical examinations accompany written assessments.
  • End-of-fifth-year examinations determine eligibility for advanced electives.

Years 6–7

  • Students specialize through advanced elective courses.
  • Independent magical research becomes a significant component of study.
  • Final-year students complete a supervised magical dissertation or capstone project before graduation.
