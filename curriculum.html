Academics

Curriculum

At Mayantraset, every student follows a comprehensive magical education during their first five years, building a strong foundation in spellwork, magical theory, history, and the natural magical world. Beginning in their sixth year, students may tailor their education by selecting advanced elective courses aligned with their talents, ambitions, and future professions.

Core Subjects

Every student completes the following eight subjects during their first five years, establishing the knowledge and practical experience expected of every graduate of Mayantraset.

Spellcasting & Magical Theory

The foundation of magical education, covering wandwork, spell structure, magical principles, and the theory behind successful spellcasting. Students learn the mechanics of channeling magical energy, precision in casting, spell construction, and the relationship between intent, focus, and magical power.

Defence Against the Dark Arts

Defensive magic, counter-curses, duelling techniques, protective enchantments, and the recognition of Dark Magic. Practical instruction emphasizes quick thinking, magical resilience, teamwork, and the responsible use of defensive spells in dangerous situations.

Potions & Alchemy

Potion brewing, magical ingredients, transmutation, and the fundamental principles of alchemical magic. Students study both traditional potion-making and the theoretical foundations of magical transformation, learning precision, patience, and experimentation.

Magical Herbology

The cultivation, identification, and magical properties of enchanted flora from Thailand and across the wizarding world. Coursework includes greenhouse cultivation, harvesting techniques, medicinal plants, and the role of magical flora in potions and spellcraft.

Magical Creatures

The study, care, behaviour, and conservation of magical beasts and spirits. Students gain practical experience handling magical creatures while exploring their habitats, magical biology, and cultural significance throughout Asia.

Ancient Magic & Runes

Ancient magical scripts, runic inscriptions, forgotten enchantments, and historical magical civilizations. Students learn to interpret magical symbols, translate ancient texts, and understand the enduring influence of early spellcraft on modern magic.

Divination & Celestial Studies

Prophecy, astrology, magical astronomy, lunar cycles, celestial magic, and divination techniques. Lessons explore how the heavens influence magical practice through observation, interpretation, and ritual while encouraging thoughtful analysis rather than blind certainty.

History of Magic

The history of magical societies, international wizarding relations, magical conflicts, and the evolution of magical law. Particular emphasis is placed on the rich magical heritage of Southeast Asia and the interconnected histories of magical communities throughout the region.

"Magic is not merely learned—it is cultivated through discipline, curiosity, compassion, and a lifelong pursuit of knowledge."

Advanced Electives

Beginning in the sixth year, students are encouraged to specialize by selecting advanced courses aligned with their talents, aspirations, and intended magical professions.

🪷 Sacred Enchantments

Protective charms, talismans, magical wards, blessings, and sacred inscriptions. Students study ancient protective traditions, ceremonial magic, barrier construction, and defensive enchantments used by magical communities throughout Asia.

⚗️ Advanced Alchemy

Magical transmutation, magical metallurgy, rare materials, and advanced alchemical theory. Coursework explores the transformation of magical substances, enchanted metals, and the creation of powerful alchemical compounds.

🗺 Curse-Breaking & Magical Archaeology

Exploration of ancient temples, forgotten civilizations, cursed ruins, relic recovery, and magical preservation. Students learn to identify magical traps, decipher protective enchantments, and safely recover historical artifacts.

🎭 Illusion Magic

Advanced glamour magic, concealment, deception spells, perception manipulation, and illusion-based enchantments. The course examines both practical applications and the ethical responsibilities involved in altering perception.

🌧 Elemental Magic

The relationship between spellcasting and the natural elements, including weather manipulation, elemental affinities, environmental magic, and the balance between magical power and the natural world.

🏺 Magical Artifact Crafting

Designing and enchanting magical tools, relics, enchanted jewelry, talismans, and magical objects. Students combine craftsmanship with advanced enchantments to create durable magical instruments.

🌏 Wizarding Cultures & Languages

Ancient magical languages, regional magical traditions, and the diverse wizarding cultures found throughout Asia. The course emphasizes diplomacy, historical understanding, and cross-cultural magical cooperation.

🌙 Soul Magic & Magical Bonds

The study of magical oaths, familiar bonds, Soul Bonds, magical contracts, and life-force connections.

Restricted Course

🐉 Advanced Magizoology

Breeding, healing, conservation, habitat management, and advanced behavioural studies of magical creatures beyond the standard curriculum, with extensive practical fieldwork.

💎 Crystal & Gem Magic

Magical gemstones, focusing stones, ley-line amplification, crystal enchantments, and the interaction between minerals and magical energy.

Senior students continue to broaden their education through highly specialized disciplines, many of which prepare them for professional careers or advanced magical research.

🧭 Magical Navigation & Portkey Theory

Apparition theory, international magical travel, enchanted maps, magical transportation networks, Portkey construction, and navigation across magical territories. Students study the principles governing safe magical travel while exploring the world's hidden magical routes.

🌿 Healing Magic

Diagnostic spells, restorative charms, magical anatomy, potion-assisted medicine, emergency healing, and the ethical responsibilities of magical healers. Practical instruction emphasizes both precision and compassion.

⚖️ Wizarding Law & Ethics

International magical legislation, Ministry governance, magical rights, courtroom procedure, and the ethical application of magic. Students examine landmark cases and the evolving relationship between law and magical society.

🪞 Mind Magic

Occlumency, Legilimency, dream magic, memory charms, and mental defense. The course explores the complexities of the mind while emphasizing responsible practice and strict ethical safeguards.

Restricted Course

⭐ Astral Magic

Lunar rituals, planetary alignments, eclipses, magical calendars, constellations, and the influence of celestial movements upon magical practice throughout history.

🖋 Spell Creation & Experimental Magic

Designing original spells, magical research methodology, controlled experimentation, spell refinement, and innovation. Students undertake supervised research projects aimed at advancing modern magical knowledge.

Final-Year Students Only

🏛 Politics of the Wizarding World

International magical governments, diplomacy, treaties, magical institutions, historical conflicts, and the political relationships that shape the global wizarding community.

🌸 Magical Botany

An advanced continuation of Herbology focusing on extinct magical flora, enchanted ecosystems, magical conservation, and the application of rare plants within advanced spellcraft and potion-making.

🕰 Temporal Magic

A theoretical course examining Time-Turners, paradoxes, historical magical chronology, and the philosophy of time. Practical manipulation of time remains strictly prohibited under international magical law.

Theoretical Course

Academic Structure

The curriculum is carefully structured to provide every student with a broad magical foundation before allowing them to pursue specialized fields of study during their final years at Mayantraset.

Years 1–5

  • Students complete the full core curriculum.
  • Every subject combines practical instruction with written theory.
  • Field expeditions, laboratory work, magical creature studies, and collaborative projects form an essential part of classroom learning.
  • Comprehensive Fifth-Year Examinations determine eligibility for advanced elective courses.

Years 6–7

  • Students build individualized timetables by selecting advanced elective subjects.
  • Independent magical research becomes a central component of study.
  • Many electives include internships, expeditions, Ministry placements, or specialist apprenticeships.
  • Before graduation, every seventh-year student completes a supervised magical dissertation or capstone project demonstrating original research or practical magical achievement.

The Curriculum of Mayantraset is designed not merely to teach magic, but to cultivate wisdom, curiosity, integrity, and a lifelong devotion to learning.
